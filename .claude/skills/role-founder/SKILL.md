# Role preset: founder / BD

Fit: finding the right accounts and people, warm paths to them, and personal outreach and posts that start conversations. It is not a CRM or a sequencer. You approve and send everything.

## Questions (after the common interview)
1. Who are you trying to reach: customers, partners or investors? Define each target: role, company type and size, the problem they have now, and the trigger that makes them buy or reply.
2. 5 named target accounts or people you'd love a conversation with.
3. Your network: which communities, investors or ex-colleagues can make intros?
4. What do you offer in a first conversation that's useful even if they never buy (an insight, a benchmark, a teardown)?
5. Pipeline stages you use.
6. Weekly capacity: messages and posts.

## What to write
- **North star**: *first conversations with target people per week*. Set only on the user's word: `north_star: true` and `north_star_at` on the outreach row, meaning a first conversation with a target person.
- **Scorecard**:
  - target people surfaced;
  - warm paths found;
  - messages sent;
  - reply rate;
  - first conversations (★);
  - posts published.
- **Who counts**: the target definitions, verbatim.
- **Outreach stages**: `sent → replied → conversation → next_step → closed | no_fit`.
- **data/focus.md**:
  - the top target segment;
  - warm intros;
  - one post theme that your targets care about.
- **Jobs on**:
  - `scout` (weekly: target people, each with a trigger and a mutual connection if visible);
  - `inspiration` (daily: target-audience posts worth a reply);
  - `digest` (daily);
  - `weekly-review`.

## Drafting rules for this role
- Lead with their trigger (a hire, a launch, a post), not your product.
- An intro ask to a mutual must be forwardable: 3 lines the mutual can paste.
- One ask per message. No calendar link in the first message unless the user says so.

## Limits to tell the user
- Mutual connections are read from what LinkedIn shows the user; the system never messages mutuals itself.
- No enrichment tools or email finding. Public pages only.
